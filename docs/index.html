How the profit and score are calculated

Total cost = purchase + renovation + purchase closing costs + renovation contingency + holding costs + resale costs. Net profit = assumed resale price − total cost. Return on cost = net profit ÷ total cost.

Score = 70 points × return / target return + 30 points × profit / target profit. Each component is capped at its maximum and floored at zero. This rule has not been validated against historical transactions.

Enter taxes, insurance, utilities and other carrying costs in monthly holding costs. Financing, income taxes, unexpected repairs and market changes need separate review. Resale and renovation amounts are your inputs, not automated estimates.

KNOW WHERE THE EVIDENCE COMES FROM

Source health & coverage

Checking

What is not established: these records do not confirm a property is off market or for sale. Probate, title searches and licensed listing data are not connected. Unpaid tax bills are kept by bill and year; the extract does not establish that every bill is legally delinquent. Closed city cases are excluded, including some cases with filed liens.

We use published read-only data services and the county’s offered download, with bounded requests. Errors retain the last successful snapshot and appear here. A successful check means retrieval worked; it does not guarantee every source record has changed.
